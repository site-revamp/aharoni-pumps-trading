# Aharoni Pumps Trading LLC – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Aharoni Pumps Trading LLC is a Dubai-based water pump trading and supply company serving residential, commercial, agricultural, and industrial customers across the UAE. Its offerings include water pumps, GRP water tanks, solar water heaters, spare parts, technical guidance, installation, repairs, and maintenance contracts.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning Aharoni Pumps as a reliable pump supply and technical solutions provider, with application-based product discovery, stronger presentation of technical expertise, and clearer enquiry pathways.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Application-Based Product Discovery
- Organised pump products around customer applications and requirements.
- Improved the presentation of product categories, including booster, centrifugal, submersible, irrigation, swimming pool, and industrial pumps.
- Made it easier for customers to identify relevant equipment instead of navigating an extensive catalogue without clear direction.

### 2. Stronger Technical Expertise and Credibility
- Gave greater visibility to technical product selection, installation, repairs, and preventive maintenance.
- Improved the presentation of established manufacturer relationships, including Grundfos, Wilo, Ebara, Lowara, and Pentair.
- Communicated the company's role beyond equipment supply by highlighting its technical support capabilities.

### 3. Clearer Customer Enquiry Pathways
- Distinguished product quotation requests from project enquiries and service requirements.
- Improved the visibility of contact options for installation, repairs, maintenance contracts, and equipment supply.
- Made it easier for different customer groups to identify the appropriate enquiry route.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more structured industrial and technical presentation aligned with the company's pump supply business.
- **Layout:** Improved the organisation of product categories, technical services, manufacturer information, and contact sections.
- **User Experience:** Prioritised application-based navigation to simplify product discovery.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Gave greater prominence to technical expertise, product supply, and maintenance capabilities.
- **Consistency:** Created a clearer distinction between equipment categories, service offerings, and customer enquiry options.
- **Conversion Focus:** Strengthened the presentation of telephone, WhatsApp, and quotation enquiry pathways.

The redesigned homepage provides Aharoni Pumps Trading LLC with a clearer product discovery experience, stronger technical positioning, and more direct enquiry journeys for residential, commercial, and industrial customers.
