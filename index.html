Common Questions

Useful information before you enquire.

What types of water pumps does Aharoni Pumps supply?

The company's catalogue includes centrifugal, booster, submersible, sewage, drainage, circulation, HVAC, swimming pool and vertical multistage pumps, among other categories.

Does Aharoni Pumps provide installation and repair?

Yes. Aharoni Pumps lists pump installation, repair and annual maintenance contract services for residential, commercial and industrial applications.

How can I choose the right water pump?

Start with your application, required flow rate, total head or pressure, water source and installation conditions. Contact the team with these details to discuss suitable options.
